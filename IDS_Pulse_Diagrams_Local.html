MANDATORY ROLE DISTINCTION: IDS REP vs CLIENT REP
• IDS Rep (IDS Field Inspector / Quality Liaison): Employees of Integrity Driven Solutions Inc. (IDS) dispatched to assembly plant floors to perform quality inspections, rework, and log shift hours (e.g. Clarence Kuiken, Hugo Ramos, Nabil, Rogelio).
• Client Rep (Client Contact / Customer Quality Manager): Key representatives and quality contacts employed by Client Companies (e.g. Magna, GM, Stellantis, Ford) who review overtime, approve extra hours, and receive published reports (e.g. Robert Sterling, Sarah Jenkins).
