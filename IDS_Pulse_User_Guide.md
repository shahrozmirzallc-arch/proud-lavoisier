## 📱 2. Field Representatives (Mobile App Simulator)

Field Quality Representatives (QREs) use the Mobile App view to log operational data live on plant floors.

1. **Logging Suspect Material (Incidents)**:
   - Tap **"New Suspect Material"** from the home actions feed.
   - **Photo Proof & CAD Pinning**: Capture component photos or tap the interactive CAD part diagram to pin precise defect coordinates (red dot).
   - **Barcode / Part Scan**: Scan or enter part numbers (e.g. `86286761`).
   - Tap **"Release & Send"** to instantly alert quality leads and update the live dashboard.

2. **Logging Rework Hours & Quantities**:
   - Tap **"Log Rework Hours"**.
   - Input total hours worked, total pieces inspected, and OK vs. NOK (reworked) counts.

3. **Submitting Expenses**:
   - Tap **"Log Expenses"** to record travel, fuel, tolls, or meal costs.
   - Attach photo receipt proof directly from your mobile camera/file system.

4. **Correction Requests**:
   - Tap **"Shift Logs"** or **"Suspects"** tab to review previous submissions.
   - If an error was made, submit a **Correction Request** directly to the Quality Lead.

## 📊 3. Web CRM Dashboard (Admins, Leads, Accountants, Customers)

The Web CRM Dashboard gives executives, leads, and accountants full real-time visibility into field operations.

1. **Visual Defect Matrix**:
   - Interactive SVG CAD blueprint plotting defect coordinates reported across all plants in real time.
   - Hover over red defect dots to view reporting plant, QRE name, and timestamps.

2. **Accountant Workspace (Colleen)**:
   - View cumulative hours, mileage calculations, and expense entries.
   - Click on receipt photo lightboxes to verify receipts before approving claims.
   - **Exports**: Generate styled Excel ledgers (`.xlsx`), QuickBooks CSV files, or white-labeled PDF summaries.

3. **Quality Lead Operations (Donna)**:
   - Audit defect trends and duplicate reports across GM Oshawa, Ford, Magna, and Hutchinson plants.
   - Manage representative shift states and approve log correction requests.

4. **Pulse AI Copilot**:
   - Click the **Pulse AI** floating assistant in the bottom corner.
   - Pulse AI automatically audits database integrity according to your role.

## 🌗 4. Day / Night Mode & Theme Customization

- **Day / Night Segmented Toggle**: Located at the top right of the top navigation bar.
  - **Day Mode**: Dark sleek theme optimized for low-light environments.
  - **Night Mode**: High-contrast light theme (`#ffffff` white cards with slate borders) for bright office environments.
- **Color Themes**: Select between Royal Blue, Neon Violet, Emerald Green, or Ruby Red in the system settings.

## ❓ 5. Troubleshooting & FAQ

- **Q: The dashboard shows a cached error.**
  - **A**: Perform a Hard Refresh (`Ctrl + F5` or `Shift + F5` on Windows / `Cmd + Shift + R` on Mac).
- **Q: Can representatives submit defect reports without clocking in first?**
  - **A**: Yes! IDS Pulse supports 24/7 on-demand logging.
