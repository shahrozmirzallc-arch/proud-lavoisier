03 How Web Dashboard & Mobile App Connect

The integration between the Web Operations Suite and Mobile Quality App follows an automated 5-step transaction lifecycle:

Step 1

Onboarding & Rep Assignment (Dashboard → Mobile App)

Admin onboards Client, Plant, PO Hours, and assigns the Field Rep in the Operations Hub. The Mobile App automatically syncs and pre-populates the inspector's plant assignment and authorized part numbers upon login.

Step 2

Real-Time Shift Execution (Mobile App → Dashboard)

Inspector clocks in on mobile and logs inspected parts, defect counts, rework hours, and photos. The Live Command Center on the dashboard updates in real time to show active plant coverage and pieces inspected.

Step 3

QA Review & 1-Click Publishing (Mobile App → Admin → Client)

Inspector submits end-of-shift containment report. QA Director (Donna) reviews the draft in the Command Center and clicks Publish to Client Portal, instantly granting visibility to the automotive customer.

Step 4

Client Visibility & Overtime Sign-off (Client Portal ←→ Mobile App)

Client Quality Manager logs into the Client Portal, reviews certified clean lot numbers, and approves inspector overtime requests in the approval drawer. The approval status reflects back to the inspector's timesheet.

Step 5

Automated Financial Reconciliation (Dashboard Accounting)

Approved shift hours are reconciled against the PO budget cap. The dashboard calculates billing amounts ($65/hr CAD or $100/hr USD) and payroll ($32/hr CAD or $70/hr USD), generating single/batch PDF invoices and QuickBooks CSVs.
