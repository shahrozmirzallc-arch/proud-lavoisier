03 End-to-End 6-Phase Operational Lifecycle

How data flows through the entire system from initial customer onboarding to final invoicing:

Phase 1

Client & Plant Onboarding (Operations Hub)

Admin registers Tier-1 supplier (e.g. Magna), assembly plant location (e.g. GM Oshawa), purchase order number, and authorized PO budget hours cap.

Phase 2

User Provisioning & Field Rep Dispatch

Admin provisions logins for inspectors and client managers, dispatches the field rep to the plant project, and locks in agreed billing and pay rates.

Phase 3

Plant Floor Shift Execution (Mobile App)

Field inspector clocks in, inspects components, logs defects and rework quantities, captures annotated photos, and submits the shift report.

Phase 4

Super-Admin QA Review & Publishing Gate

Director of QA inspects incoming reports in the Command Center and clicks "Publish to Client Portal" to authorize client visibility.

Phase 5

Client Quality Portal & Overtime Authorization

Customer Quality Manager tracks real-time lot containment status and approves field inspector overtime requests with customer comments.

Phase 6

Financial Reconciliation & Invoicing Export

Timesheet hours are reconciled against PO budget caps; batch PDF invoices, payroll spreadsheets, and QuickBooks timesheets are exported.
