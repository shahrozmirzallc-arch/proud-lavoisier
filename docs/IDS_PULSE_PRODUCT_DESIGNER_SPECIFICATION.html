01 Executive Overview & Core Mission

Mission: IDS Pulse is the mission-critical digital operating platform for Integrity Driven Solutions Inc. (IDS), powering real-time quality containment, defect inspection, and liaison engineering across major Tier-1 automotive assembly plants (GM, Ford, Stellantis, Tesla, Magna) in the US and Canada.

In high-volume automotive manufacturing, a defect containment delay of even 15 minutes can halt an assembly line costing upwards of $50,000 per minute. The design of IDS Pulse must prioritize immediate legibility, high contrast, zero ambiguity, and frictionless speed.

02 The 4 Core User Personas

Every feature and UI element must be designed with the exact mental model of the target role:

1. Field Quality Inspector Mobile App
Staff Dispatched On Plant Floors
  • Environment: Loud plant floor, safety gloves, dirty screens, one-handed operation.
  • Key Tasks: 1-Tap Clock-in, count good/bad parts, photo markup, defect logs.
  • Design Needs: Touch targets ≥ 44px, large fonts, offline queue resilience, zero nested menus.
2. Client Quality Manager Executive Portal
Tier-1 Supplier / OEM Contacts
  • Environment: Office/laptop or tablet on plant walk-throughs.
  • Key Tasks: Track containment lots, inspect certified clean parts, approve overtime.
  • Design Needs: High-level visual KPI feeds, instant overtime action drawer, zero internal cost data.
3. QA Director / Super-Admin Command Center
Donna / Greg / Shahroz
  • Environment: Dual-monitor HQ operations desk.
  • Key Tasks: Onboard clients/plants, provision logins, dispatch reps, publish reports.
  • Design Needs: 1-Stop Operations Hub with Master Matrix table, 1-click publishing gates.
4. Finance & Billing Controller Accounting Hub
Back-Office Financial Auditor
  • Environment: Accounting spreadsheets & billing reconciliations.
  • Key Tasks: Timesheet verification, PO cap tracking, batch invoice generation.
  • Design Needs: Tabular financial drilldowns, CAD vs USD currency tags, PO cap alert bars.

04 Mandatory Enterprise Design Guardrails

Every mock-up, layout, and component MUST obey these non-negotiable rules:

Rule 1 Zero Emoji Policy (Permanent)
Emojis are strictly forbidden across all buttons, feeds, modals, reports, and badges. Use clean SVG icons from lucide-react or crisp typography.
Rule 2 Zero Dark Container Leaks (High-Contrast Light Theme)
Dark container classes (bg-slate-950, bg-amber-950) inside light surfaces are banned. All form inputs must render pure white backgrounds (bg-white border-2 border-slate-300) with dark text.
Rule 3 Zero Text Truncation for Quality Data
Ellipsis (...) is prohibited for defect narratives, containment actions, and shift summaries. Layouts must wrap text dynamically into multi-line rows.
Rule 4 Strict Separation of IDS Rep vs Client Rep
IDS Reps (Blue/Cyan staff badges) must never be conflated with Client Reps (Amber customer badges). Dropdowns must cleanly isolate field inspectors from customer contacts.
Rule 5 Location-Based Currency Assignment
US Plants & Clients evaluate in USD ($X/hr USD). Canadian Plants & Clients evaluate in CAD ($X/hr CAD). Currency tags must always be explicitly rendered.

05 Key Screen Architectures

A. The 1-Stop Operations Hub (Desktop Web) Admin Command

Unifies Client Onboarding, User Provisioning, Project Setup, and Rep Dispatch into a single control surface:

  • Universal Header: Instant 1-click triggers (+ Onboard Client & Plant, + Create New User, Assign Rep Dispatch, Export PDF).
  • 4-Step Visual Lifecycle Banner: Step 1 (Client/Plant) → Step 2 (User Logins) → Step 3 (Dispatch & Rates) → Step 4 (Shift Reports & Portal).
  • Master Operations Matrix: Connected table linking Client, Plant Location + Currency, Scope/Part, PO Cap, Assigned Rep, Billing Rate, and Pay Rate.
B. Mobile Field Inspector App (375px Viewport) Plant Floor

Optimized for high-speed single-thumb execution on mobile screens and Android native APK:

  • Clock-In Hero: Large green shift launcher button with live running hours counter.
  • 4-Step Incident Flow: Step 1 Details → Step 2 Defect Quantities → Step 3 Action + Camera Canvas → Step 4 Client Routing.
  • Offline Sync Indicator: Persistent badge showing offline queue status with auto-sync retry.
C. Client Executive Portal Customer View

Dedicated portal for automotive Tier-1 supplier quality managers:

  • Containment Feed: Published inspection reports with real-time pass/fail counters.
  • Lot Traceability: Status indicators for certified clean lots and active containment tags.
  • Overtime Approval Drawer: 1-Click customer sign-off for inspector overtime requests.

06 Engineering Collaboration & Quality Standards

Design prototypes are seamlessly translated into production components using a centralized design system:

Component Architecture Standards
  • Atomic Components: Shared buttons, badges, modals, and tables in dedicated component directories.
  • High-Contrast Enforcement: Contrast ratio ≥ 7:1 across all themes and interactive states.
  • Responsive Breakpoints: Seamless layout scaling from mobile viewports (375px) to dual-monitor desktop (1920px).
Quality Assurance Enterprise
  • Visual Regression Guard: Automated theme contrast and modal accessibility validation.
  • Zero-Clipping Standard: Multiline text safety across all on-screen tables and exported reports.
  • Production Verification: Continuous testing and instantaneous live deployment.
