2. Field Quality Inspector (Rep) — Shift & Incident Flow

End-to-end workflow for field quality reps from clock-in to suspect material logging and shift summary.

Step 1
Shift Onboarding
Select assigned plant (e.g., AutoKabel Windsor), select supplier, and tap CLOCK IN TO SHIFT.
Step 2
Scan & Defect Log
Scan part barcode / bin QR. Auto-fills Part # (e.g. 86286761). Select area discovered & describe defect.
Step 3
Evidence Capture
Attach 3+ high-res photos (Wide, Medium, Closeup), 15s MP4 video walkthrough, and voice audio memo.
Step 4
Immediate Alert
Tap RELEASE INCIDENT REPORT. Triggers system event & logs supplier email notification.
Step 5
Rework & Clock Out
Log sorted pcs & rework hours. Submit End-of-Shift Summary & CLOCK OUT.

        D4 -->|No| D6[📦 Queue in Durable Outbox]

3. Operations Lead Supervisor (Donna) — Control & Audit Flow

Real-time command center oversight, PO budget caps, inspector dispatches, and quality approvals.

Phase A
Command Center
Monitor live active Rep dispatches, plant locations, active shift hours, and quality pass rates.
Phase B
Incident Audit
Review incoming suspect material alerts. Inspect high-res photos, play audio notes, and stream 15s MP4 video.
Phase C
Budget Telemetry
Track real-time Client Purchase Order (PO) budget progress bars, allocated hours, and remaining caps.
Phase D
Report & Export
Approve/Reject extra hours requests, perform rep handovers, and export official client PDF quality summaries.

6. Offline Resiliency & Cloud Sync Pipeline

Durable local outbox queuing, UUID tracking references, network detection, and Supabase cloud flush.

      flowchart TD
        A[📱 Rep Submits Incident/Report] --> B{Network Connected?}
        B -->|Online| C[☁️ Direct Upsert to Supabase DB]
        C --> D[✉️ Generate System Email Log]
        
        B -->|Offline| E[📦 nativeStorageService.stageIncidentLocally]
        E --> F[🆔 Generate Tracking Ref: LOCAL-INC-2026-XXXX]
        F --> G[💾 Store in localStorage Queue: ids_pulse_offline_queue]
        G --> H[🔔 Show Offline Confirmation Modal to Rep]

        H --> I[📡 Device Reconnects to Internet]
        I --> J[⚡ Trigger window.addEventListener('online')]
        J --> K[🔄 Execute flushOfflineQueue()]
        K --> L[☁️ Batch Upsert Queue to Supabase DB]
        L --> M[✅ Remove Synced Items from Local Outbox]
