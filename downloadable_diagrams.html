2. Representative Field Incident Submission Flow 
flowchart TD
    Start["Start: Open Rep Mobile App"] --> Auth["Authenticate User Session"]
    Auth --> LoadProj["Resolve Active Project Assignment"]
    
    LoadProj --> Step1["Step 1: Concern & Part Information"]
    Step1 --> S1_Inputs["Enter Defect Type, Area & Part Number"]
    S1_Inputs --> Step2["Step 2: Quality & Supplier Toggles"]
    
    Step2 --> S2_Inputs["Set Toggles: Returned to Supplier, Sort Requested, RMA Required"]
    S2_Inputs --> S2_Defaults["Defaults: Unknown / Unknown / Unknown"]
    S2_Defaults --> Step3["Step 3: Contacts & Evidence Attachment"]
    
    Step3 --> S3_Inputs["Attach Photo/Video Evidence & Select Client Quality Contacts"]
    S3_Inputs --> Step4["Step 4: Review & Send Summary"]
    
    Step4 --> TapRelease["Tap 'Release to Client Dashboard'"]
    TapRelease --> ClientVal{"Front-End Validation Check"}
    
    ClientVal -- "Fail" --> ErrorToast["Display Warning Toast & Auto-Redirect to Error Step"]
    ClientVal -- "Pass" --> RPC["Invoke Supabase RPC: release_incident_to_client"]
    
    RPC --> RPC_Check{"Server Database Resolution"}
    RPC_Check -- "Network / DB Error" --> OfflineMode["Save Draft Locally & Display Honest Retry Toast"]
    RPC_Check -- "Success" --> SyncOK["Update Badge to 'REPORT SUBMITTED / Released'"]
    
    SyncOK --> Feed["Publish to Admin Hub & Client Dashboard Feed"]

3. Admin Project Onboarding & Currency Resolution Flow 
flowchart TD
    A_Start["Admin Logs into Web Dashboard"] --> A_Tab["Navigate to Projects & Rates Registry"]
    A_Tab --> A_Form["Click 'Onboard New Client Project'"]
    
    A_Form --> A_Client["Select / Create Client Company (e.g. Magna International)"]
    A_Client --> A_Plant["Select Assembly Plant & Location (e.g. Oakville Assembly)"]
    
    A_Plant --> A_LocCheck{"Plant / Client Location Check"}
    A_LocCheck -- "United States (US)" --> CurrencyUSD["Set Currency = USD ($)"]
    A_LocCheck -- "Canada (CA)" --> CurrencyCAD["Set Currency = CAD ($)"]
    
    CurrencyUSD --> A_PO["Set Purchase Order Number & Allocated PO Hours"]
    CurrencyCAD --> A_PO
    
    A_PO --> A_Rates["Set Billing Rate & Rep Pay Rate"]
    A_Rates --> A_RepAssign["Assign IDS Field Representative (Liaison Inspector)"]
    
    A_RepAssign --> A_Prov["Auto-Provision Client Quality Manager User Account"]
    A_Prov --> A_Save["Submit Onboarding Transaction"]
    
    A_Save --> A_DBSync["Write Records to projects, plants, suppliers, rates & users"]
    A_DBSync --> A_Finish["Project Active & Live in Rep Assignment Dropdown"]
